A useful care loop

Know it. Do it. Remember it.

The interface stays simple because the record underneath is structured.

01 · PLAN

What needs doing?

Species-aware routines turn daily, weekly, monthly, and every-N-day care into a clear list.

02 · RECORD

What happened?

A keeper marks care complete once, and everyone’s phone sees the same current state.

03 · LEARN

What has changed?

History and trends make weights, feeding patterns, health notes, and husbandry changes easier to understand.

One habitat, two useful views

Shed works alongside Clarity.

A paludarium or turtle tank has terrestrial animal care and an aquatic system. Shared habitat IDs let Shed and Clarity link the same physical setup without forcing unlike records into one app.
